\subsection{数学}
	\subsubsection{生成树计数}
		% I.e. matrix-tree theorem.
		% Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirchhoff%27s_theorem
		% Test: stress-tests/graph/matrix-tree.cpp
		建 $N\times N$ 矩阵 \texttt{mat}, 对每条边 $a \rightarrow b \in G$ 执行
		\texttt{mat[a][b]--, mat[b][b]++} (若 $G$ 无向, 再执行 \texttt{mat[b][a]--, mat[a][a]++}).
		删去第 $i$ 行和对应列后求行列式, 即得以 $i$ 为根的有向生成树个数
		(若 $G$ 无向, 删去任意一行及对应列即可).

	\subsubsection{Erdős–Gallai 定理}
		% Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erd%C5%91s%E2%80%93Gallai_theorem
		% Test: stress-tests/graph/erdos-gallai.cpp
		度数为 $d_1 \ge \dots \ge d_n$ 的简单图存在, 当且仅当 $d_1 + \dots + d_n$ 为偶数, 且对每个 $k = 1\dots n$,
		\[ \sum _{i=1}^{k}d_{i}\leq k(k-1)+\sum _{i=k+1}^{n}\min(d_{i},k). \]
